The Moon

The black night has never been this black since that happened. Sometimes I wonder what would have become of me had it not happened. There is no answer to that anymore. I am left with the knowledge of it, and with something I cannot name that has remained ever since. The moon used to make the darkness bearable. Now it only makes it visible. I stay beneath it because there is nowhere else to go. And because, whatever happened that night, it is too late to undo it.
